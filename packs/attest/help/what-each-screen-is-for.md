# What each screen is for

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get ready** | The plan from install to an issued report: what to do next, and how far you have got |
| **Dashboard** | What is late, what is due soon, and what is missing, in one place |
| **Calendar** | Every date: policy reviews, vendor reviews, training, the next SOC report |
| **Scope** | The system description and your service commitments, then which Trust Services categories and SOC 1 objectives are in the report, and the reason for each one left out |
| **Criteria** | The 61 SOC 2 criteria and the SOC 1 control objectives, with owners, status, evidence and tests. Each one explains what it asks for, what to do, what the auditor tests and the records to keep |
| **Evidence** | Screenshots, exports, tickets and documents that prove a control worked, dated and linked to criteria |
| **SOC reports** | Each SOC 1, SOC 2 or SOC 3 report: type, period, CPA firm, opinion, exceptions and when the next one is due |
| **Risks** | Your risk assessment: what could go wrong, scored, owned and linked to the criteria that treat it |
| **Assets** | The systems inside the boundary of the report |
| **Tasks** | Work outstanding, with owners and dates |
| **Incidents** | Security incidents, how bad, and where they stand |
| **Exceptions** | Failed tests and auditor findings, and what fixed them |
| **Policies** | Your policies with versions, approvals and review dates. The **Templates** tab has 13 starter documents (the system description and the policies a SOC 2 auditor expects) that you fill in here and download as Word files |
| **Vendors** | Suppliers and subservice organisations, the SOC report or other assurance they gave, and when you last reviewed it |
| **Training** | Who completed security training and when it is due again |
| **Internal audits** | Readiness assessments, internal control reviews and management reviews |
| **Reports** | Your organisation name and logo, and the PDFs: the executive summary for the board, the readiness plan, the SOC 3 draft, the SOC 2 criteria matrix, the SOC 1 objectives matrix and more |
| **Gap analysis** | What is not done yet, and what is claimed without proof |
| **Users** | Who can sign in, and their role |
| **Audit trail** | Who changed what, and when. Nothing here can be changed |
| **Backups** | Take one, download one, restore one |
| **Settings** | Email, HTTPS, updates |
