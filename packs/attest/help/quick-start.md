# Quick start

Fifteen minutes from a fresh install to knowing what to do next.

## 1. Sign in and add people (5 minutes)

Add the people who run controls: engineering, IT, HR and security. See **Your
first hour** for roles.

## 2. Set the scope (3 minutes)

Open **Scope**. Security is always in. For each other category, either keep it
or mark it out of scope with a reason, for example "No processing integrity
commitments in customer contracts". Most first reports are Security only, or
Security and Availability.

## 3. Describe the system (5 minutes to start)

On **Scope**, fill in the two boxes at the top:

- **The system and its boundaries**: the service customers buy, and the
  infrastructure, software, people, procedures and data behind it.
- **Service commitments and system requirements**: what you promise, such as
  uptime and how customer data is protected, and what the system has to do to
  keep those promises.

Both go straight into the SOC 3 draft.

## 4. Give the criteria owners (2 minutes to start)

Open **Criteria**. Pick a category, open each criterion and set an owner. Each
one tells you what to do and what evidence the auditor will want.

## What happens next

- **Get ready** shows the plan, stage by stage.
- **Dashboard** shows anything late or missing.
- Owners get emails when their reviews are due.
- **Reports** gives your CPA firm the criteria matrix, and your customers a
  SOC 3 once the report is issued.
