# Your first hour

Six things, in this order. None of them takes long.

## 1. Add the people who keep the records

**Users → Add someone.** Add the people who look after personal data in each
department: HR, sales, marketing, IT, finance. Give each person the smallest
role that lets them do their job.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Everything, plus adding people and changing settings |
| Contributor | Read and change all records. Most people |
| Auditor | Read everything, including the audit trail. Change nothing |
| Read only | Read everything. Change nothing |

## 2. Turn on email

**Settings → Outgoing email.** Put in your mail server and send yourself a test
message.

Without it nobody hears that a request is due, that a breach clock is running,
or that a record needs its yearly review.

## 3. Start the data map

**Data map → Templates.** Pick the activities you recognise, such as staff
payroll, customer orders or CCTV, and press **Add**. Each one lands as a
draft for you to correct.

Then use **Data map → Questionnaire** with each department head, one
department at a time.

## 4. List your processors

**Processors and suppliers.** Add every supplier that stores or sees personal
data for you. Upload each signed data processing agreement (DPA).

## 5. Put your logo on reports

**Reports → Your logo.** The accountability report may go to a regulator.

## 6. Keep a backup somewhere else

**Backups → Download** the newest one, and keep that file off this server.

After that, open **Get ready**. It shows what to do next.
