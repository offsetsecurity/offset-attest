# Common questions

## Does this scan my systems for personal data?

No. Every record is written by a person. Nothing reads your other systems, and
nothing leaves this server. That keeps it simple and safe to install.

## Is this legal advice?

No. It helps you keep the records GDPR asks for and reminds people when
something is due. For hard questions, ask a data protection lawyer or your
Data Protection Officer.

## What is the difference between a controller and a processor?

The **controller** decides why and how personal data is used. That is usually
you. A **processor** handles data on your behalf, like a payroll company or a
cloud host. You need a contract (a DPA) with each processor.

## Which lawful basis should I choose?

The one that fits. Paying staff is a **contract** and a **legal obligation**.
Keeping tax records is a **legal obligation**. A marketing newsletter is
usually **consent**. Security CCTV is usually **legitimate interests**. Pick
the main one and write your reasons in the notes.

## When do I need a DPIA?

When processing is likely to be high risk to people. The screening questions
on a DPIA help: if two or more apply, do a full DPIA.

## How long do I have to answer a request?

One month from the day it arrives. If it is complex, you can extend by two more
months, but you must tell the person, with the reason, within the first month.
Set **Extended** to Yes and the countdown changes.

## How long do I have to report a breach?

72 hours from when you become aware of it, unless it is unlikely to harm
anyone. Log it in **Breaches** straight away; the countdown starts from the
time you enter as "became aware".

## Who gets reminder emails?

The owner email on each record. Data map entries and consent records are
chased 12 months after their last review. Requests and breaches are chased as
their deadline comes.
