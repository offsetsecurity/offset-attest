# Quick start

Fifteen minutes from a fresh install to knowing what to do next.

## 1. Sign in and add people (5 minutes)

Add the people who look after personal data in each department. See **Your
first hour** for roles.

## 2. Fill in the data map (5 minutes to start)

Open **Data map**.

- The Templates tab has ready-made entries for common activities: payroll,
  recruitment, customer orders, marketing lists, CCTV, website analytics and
  more. Tick the ones you do and press **Add**.
- The Questionnaire tab goes department by department. Pick a department, answer
  yes or no to each activity, add the owner, and press **Add to the data map**.

Every new entry is a **Draft**. Open it, check each field, and set it to
**Active** when it is right.

## 3. Mark what is risky (2 minutes)

On each entry, set **High risk?**. Anything marked Yes needs a DPIA. The
dashboard will remind you.

## 4. Add your processors (3 minutes)

In **Processors and suppliers**, add each supplier that handles personal data.
Set the DPA status and upload the signed agreement.

## What happens next

- **Get ready** shows the plan, stage by stage.
- **Dashboard** shows anything late or missing.
- Owners get emails when their records are due.
- **Reports → Accountability report** gives a regulator everything in one PDF.
