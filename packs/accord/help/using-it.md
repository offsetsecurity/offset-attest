# Using it day to day

Filling in the records once is not the hard part. Keeping them true is.

## Every week

- Look at the **Dashboard**. Anything red is late. Anything amber is due within
  a week. Anything grey is missing, such as a DPIA or a signed DPA.
- Log new **Requests** and **Breaches** the day they arrive. The clocks start
  then, whether or not they are logged.

## When something changes

- **A new system, app or supplier:** add or update the data map entry. If it
  might be high risk, start a DPIA before it goes live.
- **A new supplier that sees personal data:** add it to Processors and
  suppliers, and get a signed DPA before you send any data.
- **A new form that asks for consent:** add a consent record with the wording
  and a screenshot.
- **A changed privacy notice:** change the version number on the notice.
  The old version is kept in its history.

## Every year

Each data map entry and consent record has a **Last reviewed** date. Twelve
months after it, the owner gets a reminder. Open the entry, check every field
is still right, and set **Last reviewed** to today.

Review your privacy notices, DPIAs and supplier contracts on the dates you set
for them.

## When someone asks for proof

**Reports → Accountability report** puts the data map, DPIAs, requests,
breaches, processors, consent and notices in one PDF. The **Record of
processing activities** report is the Article 30 record on its own.
