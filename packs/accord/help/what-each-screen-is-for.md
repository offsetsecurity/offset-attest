# What each screen is for

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get ready** | The plan: what to do next, and how far you have got |
| **Dashboard** | What is late, what is due soon, and what is missing, in one place |
| **Calendar** | Every date: request deadlines, breach clocks, reviews |
| **Data map** | Every way you use personal data: why, the lawful basis, where it is kept, who gets it, how long you keep it. GDPR calls this the record of processing activities (Article 30). Has a Templates tab and a Questionnaire tab to help fill it in |
| **DPIAs** | Impact assessments for risky processing. Starts with screening questions, then a risk grid |
| **Processors and suppliers** | Who handles data for you, their signed agreement (DPA), and whether data leaves the EU |
| **Consent records** | Where you ask for consent, the wording, and where the proof is kept |
| **Notices and policies** | Privacy notices, cookie notices and policies, with versions and review dates |
| **Requests** | Requests from people about their own data, with a one-month countdown |
| **Breaches** | Every personal data breach, with a 72-hour countdown to tell the regulator |
| **Duties** | The GDPR duties as a checklist, with owners and evidence |
| **Evidence** | Documents that prove what you do |
| **Risks** | What could go wrong, scored, owned and treated |
| **Tasks** | Work outstanding, with owners and dates |
| **Reports** | PDFs, including the accountability report for a regulator |
| **Users** | Who can sign in, and their role |
| **Audit trail** | Who changed what, and when. Nothing here can be changed |
| **Backups** | Take one, download one, restore one |
| **Settings** | Email, HTTPS, updates |
