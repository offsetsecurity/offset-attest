# Offset Attest

**For SOC 1, SOC 2 and SOC 3.** Get ready for the report your customers ask
for, keep the evidence the CPA firm samples, and keep the report current. On
your own server.

## Who it is for

Service organisations whose customers ask for a SOC report: a SaaS company
facing its first SOC 2, a payroll or billing provider that needs a SOC 1, a
security team keeping a Type II current year after year, and the consultants
who support them.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **Scope** | The system and its boundaries, your service commitments, and which Trust Services categories and SOC 1 objectives are in the report, with a reason for each one left out |
| **Criteria** | All 61 SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and 12 starter SOC 1 control objectives, each with plain-English guidance: what it asks for, what to do, what the auditor tests, and the records to keep |
| **Testing** | Record your own tests on every criterion. A failed or partial test is flagged until it is fixed and tested again |
| **SOC reports** | Every SOC 1, SOC 2 and SOC 3 report, Type I or Type II: CPA firm, period, opinion, exceptions and when the next one is due |
| **Vendors** | Subservice organisations and other vendors, the SOC report or other assurance they gave, and when you last reviewed it |
| **Evidence** | Proof, dated and linked to the criteria it supports, so a request list becomes a download |
| **Risks and assets** | The annual risk assessment, including fraud, and the systems inside the boundary. 80 sample assets and 50 sample risks to start from |
| **Policies, training, internal audits, exceptions** | The records the common criteria are tested against |
| **Reminders** | Owners are emailed as policy, vendor, training and report dates come |
| **Get ready** | A plan of 8 stages from install to an issued report, most of which tick themselves from your records |
| **Reports (PDF, with your logo)** | SOC 3 draft, SOC 2 criteria matrix, SOC 1 objectives matrix, readiness plan, gap report, risk register, evidence register |
| **Help and Documentation** | SOC reports in plain words, Type I and Type II, and working with your auditor |

Version 1 records what people write down. It does not scan or connect to your
other systems.

## Every Offset product also has

HTTPS with your certificate · four roles, including a read-only Auditor role for
your CPA firm · every change recorded in an audit trail that auditors can read
and download · nightly backups and restore from the screen · signed one-click
updates · email reminders and a daily digest · Forgot password for
administrators.

## How it installs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | One installer. Nothing else to install |
| **Linux** | A package that installs as a system service. Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Amazon Linux, SUSE |
| **Docker** | Pull the image and run it with Compose |
| **Needs** | A 64-bit server, about 300 MB of disk plus your documents, and one port. It uses under 150 MB of memory at rest |
| **Sized for** | Up to about 50 users |

## Your data stays yours

It runs on your server, inside your network. No cloud, no telemetry, no
licence server. Offset Security has no access to it. Details are in the
security overview and the privacy sheet.
