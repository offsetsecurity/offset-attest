# Offset Accord

**For the EU GDPR.** Keep the records the Regulation asks for, meet its
deadlines, and show a regulator everything in one report. On your own server.

## Who it is for

Organisations that handle personal data about people in the EU and need to
show they comply: from a small company starting its first data map to a
privacy team with a Data Protection Officer, and the consultants who support
them.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **Data map** | The record of processing activities (Article 30): purpose, lawful basis, data, storage, recipients, transfers, retention and an owner for each entry. 22 ready-made templates and a department-by-department questionnaire |
| **DPIAs** | Nine screening questions, a risk grid with what you will do about each risk, the left-over risk, and the decision. Linked to the data map |
| **Requests** | Access, erasure, correction, objection and other requests, with a one-month countdown and the two-month extension rule |
| **Breaches** | A breach log with a 72-hour countdown to tell the regulator, and the decision on telling the people affected |
| **Processors and suppliers** | The signed data processing agreement on each processor, and how every transfer outside the EU is covered |
| **Consent and notices** | Where consent is collected, the wording and where the proof is kept; privacy notices with versions |
| **GDPR duties** | 37 duties from the Regulation as a checklist, each with plain-English guidance, an owner and evidence |
| **Reminders** | Owners are emailed as deadlines come, and when an entry has not been reviewed for 12 months |
| **Dashboard** | Everything late, due this week, or missing, in one list |
| **Get ready** | A plan of 7 stages, most of which tick themselves from your records |
| **Reports (PDF, with your logo)** | Accountability report, record of processing activities, readiness plan, executive summary, gap report, risk register, evidence register |
| **Help and Documentation** | GDPR in plain words, doing a DPIA, and handling requests and breaches |

Version 1 records what people write down. It does not scan or connect to your
other systems.

## Every Offset product also has

HTTPS with your certificate · four roles · every change recorded in an audit trail that auditors can read and download · nightly
backups and restore from the screen · signed one-click updates · email
reminders and a daily digest · Forgot password for administrators.

## How it installs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | One installer. Nothing else to install |
| **Linux** | A package that installs as a system service. Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Amazon Linux, SUSE |
| **Docker** | Pull the image and run it with Compose |
| **Needs** | A 64-bit server, about 300 MB of disk plus your documents, and one port. It uses under 150 MB of memory at rest |
| **Sized for** | Up to about 50 users |

## Your data stays yours

It runs on your server, inside your network. No cloud, no telemetry, no
licence server. Offset Security has no access to it. Details are in the
security overview and the privacy sheet.
